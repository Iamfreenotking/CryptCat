CryptCat Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 25, 2026

CryptCat is designed as an offline file encryption and decryption tool.

Data Collection

CryptCat does not collect personal data.

The app does not require an account, does not use advertising, does not use third-party analytics, and does not track users across apps or websites.

Files, Photos, Videos, and Documents

When you choose a file, photo, video, audio file, PDF, text file, archive, or other document, CryptCat processes that item locally on your device for encryption or decryption.

Your selected files and generated outputs are not uploaded by CryptCat. They remain on your device unless you manually save or share them using iOS system features.

Passwords and Keys

Passwords are used locally to encrypt or decrypt files. CryptCat does not upload, store on a server, sell, or share your passwords or encryption keys.

Important: CryptCat cannot recover lost passwords.

Internet Access

The core encryption and decryption features do not require an internet connection.

Children

CryptCat is a general utility app and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact support@bullbullgo.com.

Changes

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
