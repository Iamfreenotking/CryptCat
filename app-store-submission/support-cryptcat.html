CryptCat Support

CryptCat is an offline file encryption and decryption app for iPhone and iPad.

Contact

Email support: support@bullbullgo.com

Basic Use

To encrypt a file, open CryptCat, choose Encrypt, select a file, enter the same password twice, then save or share the generated .crypt3 file.

To decrypt a file, open CryptCat, choose Decrypt, select a .crypt3 file, enter the original password, then save or share the restored file.

Password Recovery

CryptCat cannot recover lost passwords. Keep your password in a safe place.

Offline Use

The core encryption and decryption features work offline and do not require an internet connection.
