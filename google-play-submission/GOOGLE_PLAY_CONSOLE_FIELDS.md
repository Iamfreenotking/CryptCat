### Short Description

### Full Description

CryptCat is an offline file encryption tool for Android, iPhone, iPad, and the web.

Use it to encrypt and decrypt common file types, including photos, videos, audio files, PDF documents, TXT files, ZIP archives, and office documents. CryptCat works locally on your device. Your files, passwords, and encryption keys are not uploaded to a server.

Core features:

- Encrypt and decrypt files offline.
- AES-256-CBC compatible .crypt3 file workflow.
- Automatic language detection on first launch.
- 100-language interface registry with manual language switching.
- Local file save and user-initiated share/export actions.
- No account, no ads, no analytics, and no internet permission in the Android app.

CryptCat is designed for users who need a simple local encryption workflow and want their password and files to stay on their own device.
